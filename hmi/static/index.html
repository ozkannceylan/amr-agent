Every value here is drawn with the age of the datum it came from, and nothing on this pane is ever drawn as current. The localization publishes only when its filter updates, so a standing vehicle produces no pose at all — which is why a growing age is the honest reading and a still marker would not be. Two ages are shown and never added: the monitoring service’s arrival age, on its clock, and this page’s own fetch age, on ours.

The whole warehouse map, never a crop. Free, occupied and unknown are the occupancy grid’s own three values — unknown is drawn as unknown. Lidar returns are shown in the three classes the sensor reports them in and are classified no further: nothing here is called near, far, relevant or dangerous, and no verdict, no quality judgement and no safety statement is made on this pane.
