   WHAT THE THRESHOLD COSTS IN DETECTION. A channel that freezes
   while the vehicle runs shows |d| equal to the speed itself, so
   the comparison sees a frozen channel only above 0.030638 m/s.
   Below that speed the comparison is blind to it, and what covers
   that regime is the motion-present stand-in, not this threshold.
