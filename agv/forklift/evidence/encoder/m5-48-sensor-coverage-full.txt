A 2D scan plane is only occluded by what intersects it. This
model's pallet clears the 0.15 m plane once the tines are above
0.06 m of travel, and the tines themselves clear it above 0.10 m.
That is a property of a rectangular pallet with a flat deck; a
real load overhangs, sags and is strapped, and ISO 3691-4 treats
load occlusion in the load direction as a residual to be handled
by field switching and speed, not by mounting.
